## Analysis and Models

Building robust data pipelines to explore, clean and model experimental or simulated datasets, regardless of data type: time series, images, spectra or signals.

### Approach

- **Solid statistics** — Uncertainty estimation, hypothesis testing, metrics adapted to weak signals
- **Interpretable models** — Regularized regressions, anomaly detection, dimensionality reduction
- **Reproducibility** — Clean notebooks, version tracking and parameter traceability

### Experience

This type of work is at the core of my PhD, particularly around statistical detection of faint signals in high-contrast interferometric data.

### Tools

Scientific Python (NumPy/SciPy, pandas), clear visualization (Matplotlib, Seaborn), ML models when they bring measurable value.
