## Analysis and Models

Building robust data pipelines to explore, clean and model experimental or simulated datasets.

### Approach

- **Solid statistics** - Uncertainty estimation, hypothesis testing, metrics adapted to weak signals
- **Interpretable models** - Regularized regressions, anomaly detection, dimensionality reduction
- **Reproducibility** - Clean notebooks, version tracking and parameter traceability

### Tools

Scientific Python (pandas, NumPy/SciPy), clear visualization (Matplotlib, Seaborn), initial ML models when they bring measurable value.
