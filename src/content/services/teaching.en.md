## Personalized Support

Lessons tailored to each student, whether to strengthen fundamentals, prepare for exams or dive deeper into a subject. Available both in-person and online, from middle school to first-year university.

### Subjects

- **Mathematics** — Algebra, trigonometry, calculus, probability, statistics,...
- **Physics** — Mechanics, optics, electromagnetism, quantum mechanics,...
- **Computer Science** — Algorithms, Python, data structures, introduction to scientific programming, ...

### Experience

I worked as a tutor at **Cours Ado** (Grasse, France) and on the **Compétude** platform. I enjoy finding playful ways to approach problems, with real-world scenarios tailored to each student's interests — the idea being to bring them into their comfort zone, put them at ease and build their confidence. The goal is not to do the work for the student, but to give them the tools to understand and progress independently.
