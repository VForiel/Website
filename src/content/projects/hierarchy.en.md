Hierarchy is a multiplayer PvP Minecraft map inspired by MOBAs. It features 5 different game modes, 4 detailed environments, and 24 unique hero classes, each equipped with 3 distinct spells.

Designed to be easy to pick up while maintaining the strategic depth of a MOBA, Hierarchy stands out with its gameplay diversity and focus on team play. Numerous variables are handled dynamically to ensure a smooth experience.
