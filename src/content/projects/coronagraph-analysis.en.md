Research work on the comparative analysis of several types of coronagraphs (Lyot, vortex, PIAA) in the context of exoplanet detection.

## Objectives

- Evaluate the theoretical performance of different architectures
- Quantify the impact of optical aberrations
- Identify optimal configurations according to target type

## Methodology

Complete numerical simulations including Fraunhofer propagation, coronagraphic masking, and the impact of adaptive optics.

## Results

Publication of a synthesis article comparing achievable contrasts and recommending adapted observation strategies.
