HELIOS is a simulation framework developed as part of my PhD thesis. It enables complete modeling of the astronomical observation chain, from the celestial source to the detector.

## Features

- Simulation of celestial scenes (stars, exoplanets, sky background)
- Modeling of complex optical instruments
- Realistic light propagation
- Generation of synthetic images including noise
- Modular and extensible architecture

## Technologies

Python, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, Astropy

## Impact

Used for the design and validation of high-contrast imaging instruments, contributing to the optimization of observation strategies for exoplanet detection.
